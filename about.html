Our story

Jewellery shaped by Indian heritage, made for now

TAVISH & CO. is a small jewellery house in New Kalyani Nagar, Pune — building anti-tarnish essentials, Korean-inspired edits and festive heirlooms in deliberate, hand-finished batches.

Where it began

A studio, not a showroom

TAVISH & CO. began with a simple frustration: jewellery that looked beautiful in the box and gave up after a few weeks of Pune humidity. We wanted pieces that could go from a Monday meeting to a Saturday sangeet without turning green, dull or tired.

So we started making them ourselves — small runs, protective e-coating over gold-tone plating, settings checked by hand, clasps tested before anything ships. What began as a fix for our own jewellery boxes became a house with a point of view: heritage silhouettes, contemporary proportions, honest materials.

Today our work sits in three lanes. Anti-tarnish everyday pieces you can live in. A Korean-inspired edit that reads light, layered and modern. And festive work — kundan, pearls, polki-inspired settings — that carries the weight of an occasion without the weight of an heirloom price.

How we work

Considered at every step

01

Design

Silhouettes start from Indian forms — the jhumka curve, the kada oval, the polki cluster — and get trimmed to proportions that layer easily with what you already own.

02

Craft

Runs stay deliberately small. Stones, settings and clasps are checked by hand, and nothing is bulk-packed without a final look under the lamp.

03

Every piece is e-coated for anti-tarnish protection, polished, photographed against neutral light and packed with a care card before it leaves Wagholi.

Materials & care

Honest about what we make

Our pieces are fashion jewellery: brass and alloy bases with gold-tone plating, cubic zirconia, faux pearls and glass stones — never sold as solid gold or natural diamond. The anti-tarnish coat extends their life, but they still deserve basic care.

  • Store pieces in the pouch they arrive in, away from perfume and moisture.
  • Wipe with a soft dry cloth after wearing — especially after a humid day.
  • Keep kundan and pearl work away from water, sanitiser and hairspray.
  • Layer generously, but let delicate chains rest rather than sleep in them.
